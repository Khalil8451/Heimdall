# Community code of conduct

Participants must treat each other respectfully, welcome differing experience, and critique ideas and code rather than people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are unacceptable. Respect private information and obtain consent before sharing another person's data.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and project events. Maintainers may edit/remove harmful material, warn participants, restrict participation, or ban repeated/severe misconduct. Enforcement should consider context, protect affected people, and avoid public disclosure of private reports.

Repository owner `@Khalil8451` is the proposed initial maintainer contact, based on the configured remote. A private conduct-reporting channel and an independent escalation contact are not yet configured. Until one is announced, a public issue may request a private contact without incident details; do not post personal information. Publishing a private reporting route is an owner task before community launch.

Maintainers should document the reason for enforcement to involved participants, offer an opportunity to respond when safe, and recuse themselves where a conflict exists. This is an original project policy, not a claim of adoption of an external conduct organization or enforcement service.
